# 🛒 Supermarket Sales & Performance Analysis Dashboard

An interactive retail analytics dashboard created in **Microsoft Excel**. This project evaluates transactional retail data across branch locations, product categories, customer membership segments, and payment channels to identify key profit drivers and operational growth opportunities.

---

## 📌 Business Questions Addressed
* **Total Financial Volume:** What is the total sales revenue, profit margin, and order volume across branches?
* **Product Performance:** Which product lines generate the highest total revenue vs. profit contributions?
* **Regional & Branch Impact:** Which branch (A, B, or C) and city generate the highest profitability?
* **Customer Behavior:** How do spending habits differ between **Member** and **Normal** customer types, and across payment channels?
* **Temporal Patterns:** How do sales trends fluctuate over time?

---

## 📊 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
* **Total Sales Revenue:** Overall revenue generated from store operations.
* **Total Profit:** Net profit calculated across all completed product orders.
* **Total Transactions:** Total invoice count across branches.
* **Average Order Value (AOV):** Mean monetary spend per transaction.
* **Customer Satisfaction:** Average rating across customer purchasing experience.
* **Total Quantity Sold:** Total volume of units fulfilled.

---

## 🛠️ Data Modeling & Dashboard Features
* **Pivot Table Aggregations:** Multi-dimensional summary tables analyzing sales by product line, payment method distributions, and profit contributions per branch.
* **Interactive Slicers:** Connected slicers enabling dynamic filtering across **Branch**, **Customer Type**, **Gender**, and **Date Ranges**.
* **Visual Scorecards & KPI Cards:** Custom formatted KPI metrics highlighting high-level financial health.
